      About ORBITX

      Built to make space feel close

      ORBITX is an interactive 3D Solar System explorer that runs entirely in your browser. It lets you travel through our cosmic neighborhood, from the Sun out to Neptune, and learn about each world along the way.

      Every planet orbits the Sun at its real relative speed, starting from its approximate position today. Click any planet to fly the camera to it and open a profile with its size, distance, length of day and year, temperature, moons, atmosphere and interesting facts. All figures come from NASA.

      • Explore in 3D. Rotate, zoom and fly between the Sun, the eight planets and Earth’s Moon.
      • Find any world. Search by name or nickname, or browse the planet cards.
      • Compare planets. Put any two planets side by side and see how their size, distance, days, years and temperatures differ.
      • Learn something new. Browse curated space facts drawn from NASA material.
      • Honest about scale. Sizes and distances are enlarged so everything fits on screen, and the site always says so.
